# Surface: home (single-page portfolio)

Mode: Experience. Audience: hiring engineers, recruiters, robotics collaborators (see PRODUCT.md). Job: be convinced by real engineering in under a minute, dig into one project, contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a product-visualization film of one machine, and the machine is the CV. It refuses the category default of a split hero (name left, illustration right) followed by a grid of project cards.

OWN-WORLD: A dark product-photography studio. Ground #080A0C, elevated #111417, type #F2F2EF / #8E949A, a single cool anodized accent #91B7C7 used only for live data and focus. Materials: matte graphite, bead-blasted aluminum, hairline rules at 1px rgba(242,242,239,.08). Display: Source Serif 4 at display optical size, light weight, no italics. UI: Geist. Data: Geist Mono, never tracked uppercase labels. No cards; content sits on the ground in asymmetric editorial columns.

STORY: The visitor watches a six-axis arm resolve out of darkness, understands this is an electronics/robotics engineer, follows the camera into the mechanism (capabilities), sees it exploded into the thesis pipeline, reads three projects as editorial spreads, plays with his real IK solver, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL studio; the arm occupies the right-center two-thirds at life-size scale, base cropped by the floor fade. Lower-left: name in display serif (~6rem), two role lines in Geist, tagline. Top bar: monogram left, five section links right. Primary action: "Email" link in the nav plus scroll cue; contact is one click away.

FORM: pinned brief; seed: none (pinned) — "The hero must feature a beautiful, realistic industrial robotic arm. This is the defining visual element of the entire website." / "The screen begins in a clean dark environment. Soft studio lighting gradually reveals the robotic arm." Signature interaction: scroll scrubs one continuous camera path and joint choreography; exploded view at the thesis. Motion grammar: slow exponential ease-out, 1.2–2.4s camera moves, opacity+translate 12px text reveals, nothing bounces.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
